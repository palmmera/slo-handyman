5. How booking works

  1. The Customer browses Handyman profiles and selects a Handyman who is taking requests and has finished Stripe payout setup.
  2. The Customer describes the job, may enter a preferred date, and chooses a price: either an estimate based on hours times the Handyman's listed hourly rate, or a flat price the Customer enters.
  3. The Customer pays the job price plus a $5 booking fee through Stripe Checkout.
  4. After payment, contact information is shared so the Customer and Handyman can coordinate. The Handyman can accept or decline the booking.
  5. If the Handyman declines, the Customer is fully refunded (job price plus the $5 booking fee).
  6. If the Handyman accepts, they perform the work. They may mark the job as done in their dashboard. That does not pay them; it notifies the Customer.
  7. When the Customer is satisfied, the Customer releases payment on their booking page. Only then is the Handyman's share transferred to their Stripe connected account.
  8. After payment is released, the Customer may leave a 1–5 star rating and an optional written review.

The Platform also lets anyone submit a "describe your job" request. That is a lead for matching, not a paid booking, unless and until a Customer later books and pays a Handyman through the Platform.

8. Fees: $5 booking fee and 12% commission

Each paid booking includes two SLO Handyman fees:

  • $5 booking fee. The Customer pays this in addition to the job price at checkout. It is a Platform fee, not part of the Handyman's job amount.

Example: If the job price is $200, the Customer pays $205 ($200 job + $5 booking fee). SLO Handyman retains $5 + $24 (12% of $200). The Handyman's payout after the Customer releases payment is $176.

Joining as a Handyman has no signup, monthly, or listing fee. The commission applies to booked jobs as described above. Stripe may also deduct its own processing or payout fees according to Stripe's terms; those are Stripe's charges, not additional SLO Handyman fees listed at checkout.

9. How payment works (Stripe)

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. and its affiliates. Your use of payment features is also subject to Stripe's terms and privacy policy. Handymen must complete Stripe Connect onboarding and have payouts enabled before they can be hired. Payouts go to the Handyman's Stripe connected account, and from there to the bank or payout method the Handyman set up with Stripe.

At checkout, the Customer's card is charged the job price plus the $5 booking fee. That charge is made to SLO Handyman's Stripe platform account. The Handyman is not paid at that moment. The funds remain with the Platform (processed by Stripe) until either:

  • the Handyman declines the booking, in which case we refund the Customer's full payment; or
  • the Customer releases payment after the job is accepted, in which case we transfer the Handyman's payout (job price minus the 12% commission) to the Handyman's connected account. The $5 booking fee and the 12% commission stay with SLO Handyman.

This is a held platform payment using Stripe's charge-and-transfer tools. It is not a licensed escrow service, and SLO Handyman is not an escrow agent. Releasing payment is the Customer's decision. We do not inspect the work or decide whether it was done correctly. Once payment is released and transferred, that transfer generally cannot be undone through the Platform.

Contact details are shown after a successful booking payment so the parties can coordinate. A Handyman who is marked as not taking requests, or who has not finished Stripe payout setup, cannot be booked.
